# VNP and Firewall

VPN (Virtual Private Network)
A VPN creates an encrypted "tunnel" between your device and a target server.

How it works: It hides your real IP address and encrypts all transmitted data.

Example:

Privacy: Your ISP (and anyone on public Wi-Fi) can't see what websites you visit.

Remote Access: Lets you securely connect to your company's internal network from home.

Bypassing restrictions: Allows access to region-locked content.

Firewall
A Firewall is a guard (filter) at the boundary of your computer or network that decides what traffic is allowed in or out.

How it works: It analyzes network traffic based on predefined rules.

Example:

Attack prevention: Blocks hackers from scanning and exploiting open ports on your device.

App control: Prevents suspicious software from secretly connecting to the internet.


*ISP - Internet Service Provider
